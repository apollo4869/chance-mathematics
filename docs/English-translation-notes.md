# English Translation Notes

These notes record inherited source qualifications and terminology choices. They do not amend the Chinese manuscripts or confer mathematical or historical approval on the English working drafts.

## Chapter 3 and the Brownian reading modules

- Chapter 3's characteristic-function argument invokes the uniqueness and continuity theorems supplied in later chapters. Its supplementary notices and its Appendix D remainder qualification are retained in the English translation; the argument should not be described as self-contained within Chapter 3.
- The Gauss discussion in §3.7 is explicitly a modern reconstruction with a regularity qualification for the additive functional equation. That qualification and the source's notice about the outstanding regularity discussion are retained.
- The Donsker module preserves the former Chapter 4's section numbers. Its §4.7.3 says that finite-dimensional convergence was proved in §4.5. The current main chapter's §4.5 explicitly makes its argument conditional on later characteristic-function tools. The module's opening proof-scope notice is retained; the sentence should be read with that qualification, not as a newly completed self-contained proof.
- The Wiener module retains the distinction between a construction route using extension and continuous-modification theorems and proofs of those theorems. The reflection module likewise retains the outstanding strong Markov and measure-preservation prerequisites.
- The CRT module's introductory list reviews the older manuscript, as its opening notice states. It is not a claim that the current main reading has established the CRT construction or every listed theory.

## Chapter 7 comparison

A paragraph-by-paragraph comparison found no substantive omission or mistranslation in the uniform-integrability criterion, the Vitali proof, the distinction between tightness of one measure and uniform tightness of a family, or the coupling discussion. The 440 paragraph blocks correspond, and each block retains the same multiset of inline and display formulas before the central translation of formula labels. Reordering inline formulas within a prose block follows English syntax.

## Formula label fragments

`translations/en/math-labels.json` maps the 87 unique contiguous CJK runs in the chapters, advanced modules, proof supplement and references, plus three further labels in the full writing-guidance translation. Several Chinese phrases straddle mathematical symbols. The translations are chosen for the assembled expression: “对” and “均匀” become “the law of” and “is uniform,” respectively. The isolated “上” has an empty translation because “on” is already included in the preceding phrase “There is no measure defined on”; no mathematical content is removed. Fullwidth punctuation is outside the CJK-run mapping and remains a separate typographical concern.
